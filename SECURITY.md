# Security policy
Report vulnerabilities privately to security@example.com (replace before release). Expect an
acknowledgement within 3 business days and a fix or mitigation plan within 30 days. We follow
coordinated disclosure: please do not publish details before a fix is available.
Note: bypasses of probabilistic detectors (input/context heuristics) are expected and documented in
docs/limits.md; deterministic-control bypasses (tool guard, manifest validation) are in scope.
